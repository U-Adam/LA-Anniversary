For Aurora

Open when you’re ready.

A little something for the adventure waiting for us.

Aurora,

It is still hard to put this into words.

Because there are only so many words…

and there is so much you.

With our anniversary still a week and a half away, I wanted to give you something a little different today.

Not flowers.
Not another card.

Just a reminder that my favorite thing in the world has never really been Los Angeles, movies, nice hotels, or fancy dinners.

It’s going somewhere with you.

We chased waterfalls.

We stood on the edge of the Grand Canyon while the wind tried its best to convince us to leave.

We’ve wandered cities with no plan, found restaurants we’ll probably never forget, taken wrong turns that somehow became the best part of the trip, and somehow every adventure has ended the same way:

I’m happier because you were there.
